What you get

Not a kit server. Not an MMO. Quality-of-life where it reduces busywork, scarcity where it keeps fights honest.

1PP PVPFirst-person only. Coastal spawns on Svetloyarsk, Berezino, Elektro, and Chernogorsk.
Raid weekendsRaiding allowed Friday 18:00 through Monday 06:00 server time. Walls mean something mid-week.
Single traderCustom Insight Trading Post. Relief valve for food, meds, and toppers — not a mall.
Insight jobsShort story chain and board work at Green Mountain. Survival rewards, not prestige loadouts.
Contested cachesScarce encrypted crates near military grids when the server is alive. Bring a crew.
Expansion, restrainedAI and systems stay obstacles and helpers — not status symbols.

Player hints

Green Mountain hosts Insight Cell jobs but is not a safezone. The Trading Post is the only protected market. Dig for dead-drops. Watch the weekend clock before you leave a base half-built.

Mods lean Vanilla+: Expansion, base QoL, winter atmosphere, keycard/cache events. Full config lives in this repository.
